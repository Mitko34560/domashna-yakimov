01 About us

A label that
answers its email.

MEGA HIT MUSIC is an independent record label and recording studio. We find new artists, record their songs and release them to the world.

We do three things: we record and produce music, we mix and master it, and we release it — artwork, distribution and playlist pitching included.

Our customers are unsigned singers, bands and producers, usually between 16 and 30, who have good songs but no studio, no budget and no contacts in the industry.

What makes us different is simple: we listen to every demo ourselves, we answer within two weeks, and the artist keeps the rights to their own music. We started in 2019 in a garage with one microphone and a borrowed laptop.

“A hit is not a lucky accident. It is a good song, recorded properly, and put in front of the right people.” — The wall of Studio A
02 Our core values

Four rules we
never break.

The music business has a bad reputation for a reason. These four values are our answer to it — and they decide how we treat every artist who walks in.

01

Artists first

The artist is not a product. Artists keep the rights to their songs, contracts are written in plain language, and nobody is locked in for years. If a bigger label offers them a better deal, we help them read it.

02

Craft over hype

We record real voices and real instruments, and we mix until the song sounds good on cheap headphones too. No bought streams, no bots, no fake numbers. A hit that is not real does not last.

03

Fair share

Every artist sees exactly where the money goes: streams, sales, concerts and merch. We take a fixed share, we never hide a fee, and we send a clear statement every month.

04

Brave sounds

We sign the song nobody else understands yet. Safe music is already everywhere — our job is to find the strange one that works, and to give it a real chance.
